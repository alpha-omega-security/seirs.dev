Security Engineers

Jonatan Männchen

CISO / Security Engineer in Residence

Jonatan Männchen is a software engineer and open-source maintainer with over 15 years of experience building and securing software. As CISO of the Erlang Ecosystem Foundation, he leads security initiatives across the Erlang, Elixir, and Gleam ecosystems. His work includes establishing the Ægis Initiative, operating the EEF's CVE Numbering Authority, coordinating vulnerability disclosures, and improving software supply chain security. He also contributes to open-source projects and industry standards, with a focus on making security practices practical and accessible to maintainers and developers.
