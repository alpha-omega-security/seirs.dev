Volker Dusch

PHP Ecosystem Security Team Lead

Volker is one of the PHP 8.5 release managers, a regular speaker at PHP conferences for the past 20 years, and the founding SEiR at The PHP Foundation. He helped maintain PHPUnit, has worked on language RFCs, and builds PHP performance and monitoring tooling at Tideways, where he is Head of Engineering.

Alexandre Daubois

PHP Ecosystem Security Team Lead

Alexandre Daubois is CTO at Les-Tilleuls.coop, a member of the PHP, Symfony and FrankenPHP core teams, and a security contractor for The PHP Foundation. He sits on the core team of Scrutineer, the open-source security scanner of Alpha-Omega, the OpenSSF and Linux Foundation initiative for open-source security, co-wrote Clean Code in PHP and speaks about what happens under the framework: the engine, the server, the runtime.
