Executive Director

Derek Zimmer is a security researcher and founder of OSTIF.org which is a nonprofit that helps open source maintainers improve the security of their projects through direct guidance, security audits, and improving their security tooling. Responsible for thousands of security fixes over the last eleven years, he works tirelessly to build communities of researchers and maintainers that work together to secure the ecosystems we all rely on every day.
