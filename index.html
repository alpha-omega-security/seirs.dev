SEIRs

Security Engineers in Residence

Hands-on security engineers embedded in the open source ecosystems the world depends on. They help maintainers find, validate and fix the vulnerabilities that matter, and build the capacity to keep doing so in the age of highly capable AI.

Read the role description See the impact

Why SEIRs

Open source security is under growing pressure. AI makes vulnerability discovery faster and cheaper, floods maintainers with reports of wildly varying quality, and lowers the bar for attacks on software, infrastructure and the people who maintain it. Most ecosystems have no dedicated capacity to absorb this.

Report volume

Rising volumes of AI-generated vulnerability reports, both low-quality noise and credible findings, all of which need a human to triage.

Faster discovery

AI-assisted scanning finds real bugs at scale. Someone has to validate them, explain them, and help get them fixed.

Scale favours attackers

Automated tooling lets a small number of people probe many projects at once. Defenders need coordinated capacity to keep pace.

Maintainer burnout

Maintainers need trusted, practical support, not another queue to manage. SEIRs work with them as partners.

What a SEIR does

A SEIR is contracted by an ecosystem organisation and supported by an Alpha-Omega grant. The role combines practical security engineering, trusted maintainer support, rapid response and cross-ecosystem coordination. It is not an audit or enforcement function. It is capacity.

01

Find and validate

Proactive review of ecosystem projects using human-led and AI-assisted methods. Findings are validated before escalation so maintainers only see issues that matter in practice.

02

Support remediation

Explain findings clearly, assess severity in context, help develop fixes, and support responsible disclosure and release coordination.

03

Rapid response

Act as a trusted technical resource when urgent issues arise: assess exploitability, coordinate short-term mitigation, and manage surges of incoming reports.

04

Build maintainer trust

Engage constructively and respectfully, with usable guidance and collaborative problem-solving that improves security practice over the long term.

05

Make it repeatable

Document workflows for triage, outreach, disclosure and response. Build tools, prompts and threat models so the work scales beyond one person.

06

Collaborate across ecosystems

SEIRs form a virtual team. They meet regularly, share lessons learned and build a common body of practice for securing open source against AI-enabled threats.

  • Scanning
  • Triage & dedup
  • Fixing
  • CNA operations
  • Rapid response
  • Tooling & automation
  • Registries & package managers
  • Language core
  • Community management
  • Documentation & comms

How SEIRs work

Ecosystem-first. The needs of projects and maintainers in the assigned ecosystem come first.
High-signal. Validated, actionable work over raw finding volume.
Collaborative. Maintainers are partners, not targets.
Practical. Focused on remediation and real risk reduction.
Discreet. Sensitive information is handled responsibly.
Repeatable. Methods and decisions are documented so the work can be sustained.
Cross-ecosystem. Part of a shared virtual team with common learning.
Metrics that matter. Accuracy, trust and responsiveness, never counts for their own sake.

Role description

For security engineers

SEIRs bring strong security engineering judgment, experience validating findings, fluency with AI-assisted workflows, and the ability to work constructively with maintainers. Engagements are typically six months.
